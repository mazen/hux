# Consumer assistants: UX and architecture survey

Researched 2026-10-09 for hux planning. The scope is high-level. Each claim cites its source inline.
**Verification caveat:** openai.com and help.openai.com returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches. The OpenAI facts below come from search-engine extracts of those official pages, cross-checked against press coverage. Treat them as *likely accurate but not read first-hand*, and re-check before quoting them externally. These products are days to months old, so details will move.

## 1. OpenAI "dots" (DevDay, 2026-09-29)

**What it is.** "dots" are always-on personal agents in ChatGPT, powered by GPT-6 Astra. Each dot has its own cloud computer and browser, works toward the user's goals 24/7, learns from feedback, and reaches more than 4,000 apps through plugins ([OpenAI, Introducing dots](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/)). Rollout covers Pro, Business Premium and Enterprise (beta, off by default). Pro excludes the EEA, Switzerland and the UK at launch ([ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes); [Manage dots in workspaces](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001554-manage-dots-in-chatgpt-workspaces)).

**Interaction model and continuity.** The user creates a primary dot, names it and customises it. Dots have a "bubbly", cartoonish floating-dot persona, a deliberate choice to make the agent relatable ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/)). Onboarding: create the dot on desktop or web, connect apps, and "let it introduce itself". After that it can be messaged from mobile ([Getting started with your dot](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot)). Channels are ChatGPT chat on desktop, web and mobile, a **voice call**, Slack and Teams, with SMS "coming soon" ([Introducing dots](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/)). The dot is the continuity anchor: it is one persistent entity reachable from many surfaces, not a device-bound session. It is "independent of any specific hardware or interface" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/)). *Unverified (community reports only):* a dot cannot initiate calls at launch, and the call UX has ringing delays ([community thread](https://community.openai.com/t/let-dot-initiate-opt-in-calls-including-scheduled-wake-up-calls/1402643)).

**Proactivity.** Users ask for reminders and recurring checks, for example reviewing the calendar every morning. Scheduled runs and proactive updates appear *in the conversation*, and the dot can message the user unprompted with "progress, questions, or decisions that need you". Schedules and completion alerts are managed in a "Scheduled" section and a "Recent activity" view on the dot's profile ([Getting started](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot)). Reported restriction: in proactive background mode a dot cannot send messages or modify content without authorisation ([search extract of press coverage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/); *not confirmed in official docs*).

**Consent and approval UX.** This is the most design-relevant part ([Dots privacy, security and safety FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs); [Managing app permissions](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001495-managing-app-permissions-in-chatgpt)):
- **Tiered actions.** "Take-over" actions such as changing a password or transferring money must be completed by the user. *Approve-each-time* covers actions such as permanent deletion or installing software. *Pre-approvable* actions include recurring messages. *Autonomous* actions need no approval.
- **Narrow pre-approval.** Approving one message does not grant ongoing permission to contact people.
- **Per-app permission presets:** Always ask, Allow read actions, Allow low-risk actions, and Allow all actions (flagged as risky). These are shared across ChatGPT, Codex and dots.
- **Custom Rules** in natural language, such as "never send emails". Built-in guardrails cannot be overridden by these rules.
- **Auto-review** checks the recipient and content before an email is sent, to catch a wrong address or an unintended disclosure.

**Memory.** A dot receives ChatGPT memories and recent context, forms its own memories (including from connected apps), and feeds back into ChatGPT memory. **Weak point:** users currently *cannot view, edit or delete individual dot memories*. The only reset is deleting the dot. Turning off ChatGPT Memory stops sharing but does not purge what the dot already received ([Dots FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001529-dots-privacy-security-and-safety-faqs)).

**Privacy architecture.** The design is fully cloud. The sandbox boundary is the dot's own cloud computer. The user's PC stays separate unless it is explicitly connected through the desktop app. That connection is off by default and revocable, and for Enterprise it needs admin and member opt-in, with the machine online ([Introducing dots](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/); [Getting started](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001530-getting-started-with-your-dot)). There is no on-device inference.

**Extensibility.** Plugins and apps (4,000+). "Specialist dots" have their own identity, credentials and IT-provisioned hardware (enterprise pilots). Microsoft Agent 365 provides security controls ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/29/openai-launches-dots-its-bubbly-agentic-avatar/)).

## 2. Amazon Alexa+

- **Interaction and continuity.** Voice-first, tolerant of loose phrasing, with selectable personality styles. A conversation started on an Echo or Fire TV continues on the phone, in the car or on alexa.com, and "Alexa+ remembers the context" ([Amazon: Introducing Alexa+](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-alexa-generative-artificial-intelligence); [Alexa.com](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/alexa-plus-web-ai-assistant)).
- **Proactivity.** It stays in the background and is "proactive when it's important": leave-earlier traffic nudges, price-drop alerts on wished-for gifts ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-alexa-generative-artificial-intelligence)).
- **Memory.** You can explicitly tell it things to remember (dates, diet, recipes), and you can *forward documents and emails* to it, e.g. school emails become calendar entries ([Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-alexa-generative-artificial-intelligence)). *Unverified:* a dedicated UI to review or delete Alexa+ memories. Voice History review and deletion exists in the Alexa Privacy dashboard ([Amazon Trust & Privacy](https://www.amazon.com/b?ie=UTF8&node=203453976011), fetch failed, so this comes from a search extract).
- **Privacy.** Cloud-only on AWS. Amazon removed the local "Do Not Send Voice Recordings" option in March 2025 because generative AI needs cloud compute ([The Register](https://www.theregister.com/2025/03/17/amazon_kills_on_device_alexa/)). Hardware mic and camera off switches remain.
- **Actions and extensibility.** "Experts" (bundles of APIs and instructions) cover partners such as Uber Eats, OpenTable, Thumbtack and Hue. Agentic web navigation handles partners without APIs ([Alexa+ AI-native SDKs](https://developer.amazon.com/en-US/blogs/alexa/alexa-skills-kit/2025/02/new-alexa-announce-blog)). The launch post says users need not "supervise or intervene" in bookings. *Not found:* an explicit purchase-confirmation flow.

## 3. Google Gemini (Android "Gemini Intelligence", Gemini app/Spark, Gemini for Home)

- **Proactivity.** Proactive Assistance uses on-screen content and device signals for suggestions, such as surfacing a reservation code. Users choose which on-device sources (messages, screenshots) feed it ([Android Help](https://support.google.com/android/answer/17499451?hl=en)). The opt-in **Daily Brief** distils priorities from connected apps and chats into one view, steered by thumbs up or down ([Gemini app blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/next-evolution-gemini-app/)).
- **Gemini Spark** is a cloud 24/7 agent with recurring tasks and triggers (e.g. "find new subscription fees in monthly statements"). It is "designed to ask you first before performing high-stakes actions like spending money or sending emails". Users choose which apps it connects to ([Gemini app blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/next-evolution-gemini-app/)).
- **Memory.** Personal Intelligence is opt-in, per connected app (Gmail, Photos and so on), and can be turned off at any time ([Personal Intelligence](https://gemini.google/overview/personal-intelligence/)). Gemini for Home has a deliberately short **15-minute conversational memory window**, plus explicit user-saved facts in "Ask Home", such as a nanny's name used to resolve camera queries ([Gemini release notes](https://gemini.google/release-notes/); [Google Home help](https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/15962877?hl=en)). *Unverified:* any unified memory shared between phone and Home.

## 4. Apple Siri AI / Apple Intelligence (WWDC26, beta since Sept 2026)

- **Interaction.** Voice, typing, onscreen awareness ("add the upcoming home games on this page to my calendar"), and camera input ([Apple newsroom, Jun 2026](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-introduces-siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant/); [Sept rollout](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/09/siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant-is-here/)).
- **Continuity.** Conversation history syncs privately through iCloud, so a conversation started on a Mac can continue on iPhone, Watch or Vision Pro. A Smart Stack suggestion proactively offers to resume a recent conversation ([Apple newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-introduces-siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant/)).
- **Memory.** "Personal context" is retrieval over the user's own data (Messages, Mail, Photos, plus third-party apps via Spotlight). It is an on-device index, not a separate memory store. The Spotlight index and App Toolbox run "entirely on device" (same source).
- **Privacy architecture.** On-device models first, escalating to **Private Cloud Compute**. PCC guarantees stateless computation (no retention or logging), enforceable guarantees, no privileged runtime access, non-targetability and *verifiable transparency*: published binaries and source, a Virtual Research Environment, and device-side attestation before a request is sent ([Apple Security: PCC](https://security.apple.com/blog/private-cloud-compute/); [PCC research](https://security.apple.com/blog/pcc-security-research/)). In June 2026 PCC expanded onto Google Cloud confidential-computing hardware, with the same requirements and an append-only hardware ledger ([Expanding PCC](https://security.apple.com/blog/expanding-pcc/)).
- **Extensibility.** App Intents expose entities, actions and onscreen entities (NSUserActivity, View annotations). App Schemas cover common domains ([WWDC26 App Intents](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/343/); [App Schemas](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/240/)). *Not found:* Apple's confirmation UX for Siri AI actions in the press material.

## UX patterns worth copying for hux (non-technical users)

1. **One persistent, named, friendly agent identity across all surfaces** (dots persona, Alexa personality styles). Devices are windows onto the same assistant and the same conversation, not separate assistants ([dots](https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/), [Alexa+](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-alexa-generative-artificial-intelligence), [Siri iCloud sync](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-introduces-siri-ai-a-profoundly-more-capable-and-personal-assistant/)).
2. **Tiered action policy in plain language:** do it / ask each time / pre-approve narrowly / you must do it yourself. Add simple presets (Always ask, Read-only, Low-risk, Everything) and natural-language "house rules" that can never override hard guardrails (dots).
3. **A pre-send sanity check** on outbound messages, such as the right recipient and no unintended disclosure (dots Auto-review).
4. **Proactive output lands in the conversation thread**, with one "Scheduled" list and one "Recent activity" log for inspection. A daily brief steered by thumbs up or down (Gemini).
5. **Opt-in per data source, revocable at any time** (Gemini Personal Intelligence, dots local-computer access off by default).
6. **Memory that people can see and edit.** dots' lack of this ("delete the dot to reset") is an anti-pattern to avoid. Gemini for Home's short session window plus explicit "remember this" facts suits shared household devices.
7. **Ingest by forwarding.** "Forward the school email to the assistant" is a zero-learning-curve input path (Alexa+).
8. **A verifiable privacy story.** Apple PCC (stateless, attested, publicly inspectable) is the model for hux's "local first, escalate only with guarantees". Alexa's 2025 removal of local processing shows how fragile a privacy promise becomes when it depends on the cloud.
